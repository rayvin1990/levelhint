What Is ReStory: Chill Electronics Repairs?

Set in the streets of mid-2000s urban Japan, ReStory puts you behind the counter of a small electronics repair shop. Customers bring in broken gadgets — each with their own story — and you carefully disassemble, clean, replace faulty parts, and reassemble them using your trusty toolset.

The game features:

  • In-depth device disassembly: Take apart each gadget layer by layer, inspect components, clean grime, swap broken parts
  • Y2K-era web browser: Hunt for spare parts on retro-styled marketplace websites from the comfort of your shop computer
  • Branching narrative: Your choices with customers affect their lives — and the ending you get. Report a disturbing find or help a student confess their love
  • 15 unique devices: From officially licensed Atari 2600 to parody brands like Pokia (cell phones), Nony (game consoles), and Eggotchi (virtual pets)
  • Shop customization: Arrange devices and decorations on your shelves and desk
