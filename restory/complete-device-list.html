ReStory: Chill Electronics Repairs — Complete Device List (All 15 Devices)

Updated: August 2026 • 5 min read

ReStory features 15 unique devices you can buy broken, disassemble, clean, repair, and sell for profit. They span 5 categories: game consoles, cell phones, music players, cameras, and appliances. Many are parody versions of real Y2K-era electronics — like Pokia (Nokia), Nony (Sony), and Eggotchi (Tamagotchi).

Game Consoles (8 Devices)

Game consoles are the most complex and profitable device category. They have the most internal components and highest resale value.

How to Find Rare Devices

  • Customer requests: Story characters bring specific devices for repair. These are often rare or one-of-a-kind.
  • Auction website: The Marketplace - Auction site on your shop computer cycles through listings. Check it between repairs — deals on rare devices don't last.
  • Direct purchase: Standard devices can be bought from the shop computer's marketplace at any time, but at full price.
  • Repeat playthroughs: Some devices may only appear in specific story branches. Multiple playthroughs reveal the full catalog.
