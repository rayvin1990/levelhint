ReStory: Chill Electronics Repairs — Complete Device List (All 29 Devices)

Updated: August 2026 • 7 min read

ReStory features 29 unique devices you can buy broken, disassemble, clean, repair, and sell for profit. They span 6 categories: game consoles, cell phones, music players, cameras, appliances, and laptops. Many are parody versions of real Y2K-era electronics — like Pokia (Nokia), Nony (Sony), XI-box (Xbox), and Eggotchi (Tamagotchi).

How to Find Rare Devices

  • Customer requests: Story characters bring specific devices for repair. These are often rare or one-of-a-kind.

  • Direct purchase: Standard devices can be bought from the shop computer's marketplace at any time, but at full price.
