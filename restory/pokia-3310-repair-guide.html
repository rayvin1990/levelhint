ReStory: Pokia 3310 Repair Guide — Full Teardown, Faults & Profit

Updated: August 2026 • 5 min read
Quick Answer — Pokia 3310 at a glance:
  • Difficulty: Easy — the best device to learn the repair flow on.
  • Internals: Keypad, screen, battery, antenna. Few components, no ribbon cables to rip.
  • Most common fault: Keypad unresponsive (dirt/grime under the membrane) — fixed with alcohol + cotton swab.
  • Profit: Low per unit, but fast turnaround. Treat it as filler between console repairs.
  • Real-world inspiration: Nokia 3310 (2000) — the legendary "indestructible" phone.

Why Start With the Pokia 3310

The Pokia 3310 is ReStory's teaching device. Its internals are the simplest in the game — a keypad membrane, a small LCD screen, a removable battery, and an antenna. There are no fragile ribbon cables and no multi-layer boards to misalign. If you're learning how the repair flow works (buy broken → diagnose → disassemble → clean/swap → reassemble → test → sell), this is the device to practice on.

Full Teardown — Step by Step

  1. Remove the back cover and battery. The battery pops out first — always disconnect power before working on any device.
  2. Unscrew the 4-6 case screws. Use the precision screwdriver. Track them with the on-screen screw counter.
  3. Lift the front shell. The keypad membrane sits between the shell and the board. Don't let it drop — it's the part most likely to be the actual fault.
  4. Inspect the membrane contacts. Look for grime, oxidation, or debris. This is the #1 fault point.
  5. Clean with alcohol + cotton swab. Gently wipe the gold contacts on both the membrane and the board.
  6. Reassemble in reverse. Seat the membrane, drop the shell on, replace screws, battery, cover.
  7. Run the final diagnostic. Confirm the screen lights and all keys register before delivering.
Speed tip: With practice, a full Pokia 3310 repair takes under 2 minutes. Use these as quick fillers — run one while waiting for a console's solder to cool or thermal paste to dry.

Profit Analysis

The Pokia 3310 is a low-margin, high-volume device. Individual payouts are small, but you can clear 4-6 per day between bigger jobs.

  • Typical repair payout: Low (early-game smartphone tier)
  • Parts cost: Minimal — most fixes need only alcohol and time
  • Best use: Filler repairs between console/audio jobs, or a safe income floor when you're low on cash
