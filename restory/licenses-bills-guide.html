ReStory: Licenses, Bills & Upgrades — What to Buy First, What to Skip

Updated: August 2026 • 5 min read
Quick Answer:
  • Weekly bills: Rent + Internet = 5,000–6,000 yen. Later: electricity + security added.
  • Best internet plan: Go with the 1,500 yen plan (highest tier). Devs have hinted it may matter later.
  • Upgrade priority: Screwdriver → Brush → Tip Jar → Shredder → Storage → Skip Airbrush/Toolkit until required.
  • Licenses: Buy them whenever you can. They unlock new device types, story progression, and cheaper parts from manufacturers.
  • Waste of money: Sonic bath (early game), airbrush (adds zero value), reprogramming toolkit (zero value).

Licenses: The Real Progression System

Licenses are how the game gates content. Every license you buy unlocks:

  • A new device category you can accept repair orders for
  • Direct parts ordering from the manufacturer (often cheaper than the marketplace)
  • Story progression — the main plot advances through license purchases
Key insight: The marketplace descriptions and ratings for used devices are not always accurate. You may buy a "good condition" console and discover it's a wreck inside. With a license, you order new parts directly from the source — guaranteed condition, often at a discount. Licenses pay for themselves in reduced part costs.

When to Buy Licenses

  • Buy them as soon as you can afford them. Licenses unlock more repair categories = more jobs = more income. The sooner you expand, the faster your shop scales.
  • If you need to build a cash buffer: Walk-in customers will still bring devices you don't have licenses for. You'll accept them and they'll sit in your inventory. The story won't advance until you fix them, but you can grind cleaning-only jobs in the meantime to save up for the license. This is a safe way to farm money without pressure.

Online Orders: Daily Revenue Engine

Every day, your shop computer lists 11 online orders. These are your bread and butter:

  • Mix of clean-only and repair jobs — some just need a wipe, others need parts
  • 3-day deadline — complete within 3 days or your customer rating drops
  • Pro strategy (night grinding): Between midnight and 8 AM, your shop can't send/receive anything. Use this window to crank through cleaning-only jobs (no parts needed). At 8 AM the next morning, a stack of completed orders ships out for a big cash injection. Zero risk, zero cost, pure profit.
