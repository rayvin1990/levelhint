Frequently Asked Questions

What is LevelHint?

LevelHint is an unofficial, fan-made guide site. We write practical, no-fluff walkthroughs and reference pages for the games real players search for.

Are the guides free?

Yes — every guide is 100% free. The site is supported by ads, so we never ask you to pay for a guide.

Where does your info come from?

We play the games ourselves and cross-check facts against official wikis, patch notes, and community sources like Steam forums and Reddit. When a number or mechanic comes from a single community source, we say so.

Which games do you cover?

Right now we cover two games: Fields of Mistria (10 guides) and ReStory (7 guides). We add new games and guides over time.

Is this the official site?

No. LevelHint is not affiliated with NPC Studio, Mandragora, tinyBuild, or any other developer or publisher. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.

How can I request a guide or report an error?

Email us at contact@levelhint.com. If you spot a wrong number or a broken link, tell us and we’ll fix it.
