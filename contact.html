What to write to us about

  • Report an error — a wrong number, a broken link, or an outdated mechanic. Tell us which page and what’s wrong.
  • Request a guide — a specific question you couldn’t find an answer for. We add guides based on what players actually search.
  • Give feedback — anything that would make the guides clearer or more useful.
  • Business & advertising — partnership or ad inquiries.

We read every message, but as a small team we can’t always reply to each one individually.
