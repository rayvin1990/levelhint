Fields of Mistria Gifts & Friendship Guide

How friendship points work, every villager’s birthday, and the easiest gift to grab for each.

Quick answer:
  • Talk to a villager once a day for +7 friendship (free, no cost).
  • A Loved gift is worth +20, a Liked gift +10, a neutral gift +3.
  • Gifts on the villager’s birthday get a big bonus — save their favorite for that day.
  • You can only give one gift per villager per day, but you can gift every villager daily.

How Friendship Points Work

ActionFriendship pointsNotes
Talk (once per day)+7Free, do it every day for everyone
Loved gift+20The big one — learn each character’s favorites
Liked gift+10Fallback when you don’t have their favorite
Neutral gift+3Better than nothing, but wasteful
Disliked / hated gift+0Never loses points, just wasted
Birthday giftbonus on topSave the loved gift for their birthday
Friendship never decays and never goes down. Giving a disliked gift costs nothing but the item — you just don’t gain. So there’s no downside to gifting daily, only upside.

How to Raise Hearts Fast

  1. Talk to everyone, every day. +7 per villager is free and adds up faster than you think.
  2. Gift daily, even liked items. +10 per liked gift beats skipping days. Only save the truly loved gift for the birthday.
  3. Never miss a birthday. The birthday bonus is the single biggest one-day jump, so mark dates and prepare the gift a day ahead.
  4. Use Elsie’s gossip. After the “Seeking Gossip” quest, Elsie reveals one villager’s loved gift per day — free intel.
  5. Don’t hoard gifts for a “perfect” moment. Daily liked gifts plus daily talk always beat waiting for the perfect loved item.
One gift per villager per day. You can’t dump five gifts on someone in one morning. The game also lets you gift after midnight the night before a birthday and still counts it — useful for avoiding the morning rush.
