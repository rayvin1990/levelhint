Fields of Mistria Skills & Perks Guide

How the 9 skills work, how to earn Essence, and which perks to unlock first.

Quick answer:
  • There are 9 skills: Farming, Fishing, Mining, Combat, Ranching, Cooking, Woodcrafting, Blacksmithing, Archaeology.
  • Do the activity to level the skill; collect Essence (purple wisps) and spend it at a Caldarus Statue to buy perks.
  • Perks unlock in tiers: Tier 1 at skill lv 1, then 15, 30, 45, and higher.
  • Spend early Essence on time-saving perks first — they pay back every single day.

How Skills and Essence Work

You level each skill just by doing the thing — water crops and Farming ticks up, break rocks and Mining climbs. No separate grind. As you work, purple Essence wisps drop from farming, mining, fishing, foraging, and chopping. Once Caldarus gifts you the ability to sense them (early in your first spring), you carry the wisps to his statues and trade them for perks.

The first tier is cheap and available almost immediately — grab those first. Higher tiers need skill level 15, 30, 45, and up, so the deep perks come later.

The one upgrade that matters most isn’t a perk — it’s your watering can. Pour your first ore into a bigger watering can. More coverage buys back real minutes every morning, and those minutes are the currency the whole game runs on. A Copper Pickaxe is second, so you can break the larger rocks in the mines.

The 9 Skills at a Glance

  • Farming — watering, planting, harvesting. Home of the best time-saving perks.
  • Fishing — rods, bait, and the Legendary perk (lv 30) that unlocks legendary fish.
  • Mining — better ore drops and rock-clearing perks.
  • Combat — critical hits, shields, and faster clears in the mines.
  • Ranching — feeding, breeding, and animal product boosts.
  • Cooking — the core money loop, plus stamina and speed infusions.
  • Woodcrafting — crafting returns, quality boosts, and extra materials.
  • Blacksmithing — cheaper ingots, faster crafting, and the Hasty movement perk.
  • Archaeology — better artifact drops and dig-site bonuses for the museum.
You can disable perks. Some perks hurt specific goals — for example, Aquatic Antiquities makes treasure chests show up while fishing, which you might want off if you only want fish. You can toggle perks off in the menu, so don’t be afraid to experiment.
