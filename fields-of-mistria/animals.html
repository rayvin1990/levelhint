Fields of Mistria Animals & Breeding Guide

Every animal type, how breeding works, and how to chase the rarest color tiers.

Quick answer:
  • Small animals (chickens, ducks, rabbits, capybaras) live in a Coop; large animals (cows, horses, sheep, alpacas) live in a Barn.
  • Buy animals from Hayden at Sweetwater Farm — but you need the building placed first.
  • Breed two opposite-sex adults of the same species with Heart-Shaped Treats, and a baby arrives 4 days later.
  • There are 6 color tiers; the rarest are Seasonal (tier 6), tied to the season the baby was conceived in.

How to Get Animals

  1. Buy a Barn (large animals) or Coop (small animals) blueprint from the Carpenter’s Shop and place it on your farm.
  2. Head to Hayden’s Shop at Sweetwater Farm (west of your farm) and choose “Adopt Animals.”
  3. You can’t adopt without an empty spot in a building — small barns and coops hold 4 animals, medium hold 8.

At the start you can only raise cows and chickens. Completing the late-game Upgrade Hayden’s Barn quest unlocks ducks, horses, rabbits, sheep, alpacas, and capybaras.

How Breeding Works

  1. Get two opposite-sex animals of the same species, both fully mature (coop animals ~3 days, barn animals ~5 days).
  2. Buy two Heart-Shaped Treats for that species from Hayden.
  3. Feed one treat to each parent on the same day — keep both in the same barn/coop.
  4. A stork icon appears next to both the next day; the baby is born 4 days later.

The 6 Color Rarity Tiers

Breeding favors the higher tier. Two tier 1 animals produce a tier 2 baby. Breed a tier 1 with a tier 2 and you’re likely to get tier 3. To reliably climb, breed two animals of the same tier. Tier 6 is the only one that isn’t guaranteed — it needs two tier 5 parents and some luck.

Seasonal Variants (Tier 6)

The seasonal color is decided by the season the baby was conceived in, not the season it’s born. If you feed Heart-Shaped Treats to two cows on Summer 26, the baby has a chance to be a summer variant even though it’s born on Fall 2. There are four seasonal variants, one per season.

How to Care for Animals

  • Pet + feed every day. Higher affection = more products. Small animals eat Grass Seeds or Small Animal Feed; large animals eat Hay or Large Animal Feed.
  • Let them graze. Ring the bell by the barn/coop door to send animals outside — they feed themselves on grass. Fence off your crops first.
  • Keep them out of bad weather. They love sun, hate rain and snow.
  • Animals don’t die if you miss a day — they just get grumpy and produce less.
