Fields of Mistria Cooking Guide: Kitchen Tiers, Best Recipes & Fast Leveling

Updated: August 2026 • 7 min read
Quick Answer:
  • Get a kitchen: Buy from Carpenter for 1,000t, or use the Inn's free kitchen until you can afford one.
  • 3 kitchen tiers: Tier 1 (low-level recipes), Tier 2 (mid-level), Tier 3 (all recipes). Upgrades unlock higher Cooking levels.
  • Fastest leveling: Spam-cook Bread (Flour ×1, Cooking 1) or Blackberry Jam (Blackberry ×6 + Sugar ×1, Cooking 2).
  • Best stamina food: Summer Salad (+120 HP/Stamina, Cooking 28, Tier 2 Kitchen).
  • 140+ total recipes across 7 categories: Pantry, Snacks, Salads, Soups, Main Dishes, Desserts, Drinks.

How to Get a Kitchen

You have two options:

  1. Use the Inn's kitchen (free): The Sleeping Dragon Inn has a Tier 1 kitchen anyone can use. Walk in and cook — no cost, no unlock required. Perfect for your first few weeks.
  2. Buy your own kitchen from the Carpenter: Starting at 1,000t, you can buy a kitchen for your farmhouse. You’ll eventually want one for convenience and access to higher-tier cooking.

How to Level Cooking Fast

Cooking XP is earned per dish crafted. The XP is the same whether you cook a 2-star snack or a 5-star main course, so the fastest leveling strategy is spam the cheapest recipe with bulk ingredients.

  1. Bread (Cooking 1): Flour ×1. Buy Flour in bulk from the General Store (cheap). Needs nothing else. Spam 50-100 at a time.
  2. Blackberry Jam (Cooking 2): Blackberry ×6 + Sugar ×1. Forage blackberries in Spring in massive quantities. More XP per craft than Bread, but requires foraging.
  3. Grilled Corn (Cooking 2): Corn ×1. Summer crop, plant in bulk and cook everything.
  4. Hard Boiled Egg (Cooking 1): Chicken Egg ×1. Once you have chickens, eggs are free and infinite.
Time management: Cooking takes in-game time. Use the Time To Eat I/II/III Cooking perks to reduce crafting time by 10/20/30 minutes. At Time To Eat III, bulk cooking becomes instant — you can process 100 items in one in-game minute.

FAQ

Q: How many recipes are there in Fields of Mistria?

A: 140+ recipes as of the second major update (March 2025). The number grows with each Early Access update.

Q: Can I cook without a kitchen?

A: Yes — use the Inn's free kitchen (Tier 1 only). No purchase needed. Walk in and cook anytime during business hours.

Q: What's the fastest way to reach Cooking Level 30?

A: Grow Corn in Summer — massive quantities, cheap seed, single ingredient. Spam-cook Grilled Corn with Time To Eat perks. You can reach Cooking 30 in one season.

Q: Should I cook or sell raw ingredients?

A: Cook first, sell cooked dishes. Almost all cooked food sells for more than the raw ingredients combined. The margin is usually 20-50t per dish. If you have the time, always cook.
