Fields of Mistria Fishing Guide — All Fish, Bait, Legendaries & Perks

Updated: August 2026 • v0.15.x+ • 6 min read
Quick Answer:
  • Fish in ponds, rivers, ocean, and mine levels (not the town fountain).
  • Hold & release to cast: wait for the "!" notification, then reel in. Casting uses no stamina; only catching does.
  • Schools of fish appear randomly — fish in these for rapid consecutive catches.
  • 4 legendary fish: Cherry (Spring), Lightning (Summer), Leaf (Fall), Snow (Winter). One per save.
  • Use Fish Traps with bait for passive daily catches (Blue Crab, Sea Urchin, Amberjack, Hake, Bait Thief).

Fishing Basics & Rod Mechanics

Fishing in Fields of Mistria works with a simple minigame system:

  1. Cast: Hold the tool button and aim with the block indicator. The closer to the fish shadow, the faster they approach. Red X means uncastable water.
  2. Wait: The fish will nibble several times. Ignore the first taps — wait for the "!" notification above the float.
  3. Reel in: Click on the "!" notification to hook the fish. If you reel too early (before "!"), the fish swims away.
  4. First-time catch: Each species displays its name, price, and description the first time you catch it.
Pro tip: Aim for the closest fish shadow to the shoreline. The reticle shows fish size — larger shadows = bigger (and generally more valuable) fish. You don't need different rods for different fish; one rod catches everything.

Fish Bait System

Fish bait is obtained from Terrariums (eco-boxes on your farm). Apply bait to your rod to attract specific rarity-tier fish. The rarity of your bait determines what shows up:

RareGolden EelRareRarely seen in nature. Any location.
Bait fish are season-independent: All bait-exclusive fish can be caught any season, any weather. Use bait during your target fish's off-season to keep your daily fishing productive.

Fish Traps (Passive Fishing)

Place a Fish Trap in any water body with bait inside. Check it daily — it catches items passively. Trap catches are season and weather independent.

Legendary Fish

Unlock the Legendary skill (Fishing lv 30, 80 Essence) to enable legendary fish. When the weather and season match, you'll see: "A legendary fish is nearby..."

Critical: Each legendary fish can only be caught once per save file. They sell for low amounts and are mediocre as gifts. Donate them to the Museum's Fish Wing immediately — you cannot complete the museum collection without them, and there's no second chance.

Diving (Underwater Foraging)

When swimming, look for dark underwater caves. Swim into them to collect items. Diving costs zero stamina and can yield unique fish and artifacts not available by rod. Unlock the Sunken Secrets skill to find artifacts from the Sunken Artifact set in dive spots.

Tips for Maximizing Fishing Income

  • Fish schools are money: When a school appears, drop everything and fish in it for rapid catches.
  • Unlock School Crasher (Lv 15): Swimming into a school gives you half its fish instantly with zero stamina cost.
  • Use traps as passive income: Place traps with bait in every water body you pass daily.
  • Don't sell legendaries: Museum first. Always.
  • Rare bait = Golden Eel: Save rare bait for Golden Eel, the most valuable bait-exclusive fish.
