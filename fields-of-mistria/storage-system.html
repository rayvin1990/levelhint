Fields of Mistria Storage System Guide: Every Chest, Capacity & How to Get Them

Updated: August 2026 • 7 min read
Quick Answer:
  • First chest: Basic Wood Chest (30 slots) — craft at Woodcrafting table or buy from General Store for 250t.
  • Best mid-game: Stone Storage Chest (42 slots) — crafted, requires town rank progression.
  • Best late-game: Deluxe Storage Chest (54 slots) — buy from Wheedle at Saturday Market for 10,000t.
  • For food: Lovely Cottage Icebox (42 slots, crafted) or Deluxe Icebox (54 slots, 12,000t at Saturday Market).

If you’ve been playing Fields of Mistria for more than a few hours, you already know the problem: your inventory fills up fast, and you have nowhere to put anything. Farming, mining, fishing, foraging — every activity generates items, and without a solid storage system, your farm turns into a mess of dropped items on the floor.

Here’s the complete breakdown of every storage container in the game, how to get them, and how to organize your stuff efficiently.

How to Get Your First Storage Chest (Beginner Guide)

When you start Fields of Mistria, you have zero storage. Your backpack fills up within minutes. Here’s exactly what to do:

  1. Gather wood — Chop a few trees on your farm. You only need basic wood, not hardwood.
  2. Visit the Carpenter’s shop — Interact with the Woodcrafting table. The Basic Wood Chest recipe should be available from the start (no skill level requirement).
  3. Craft it — If you have the materials, craft the chest right there.
  4. Or just buy one — Go to the General Store and buy a Basic Wood Chest for 250t. This is the fastest option if you have some gold from the first few days.

Place the chest anywhere on your farm. You can pick it up and move it later by hitting it with a tool while it’s empty.

Storage Upgrade Path: 30 → 42 → 54 Slots

The storage progression in Fields of Mistria goes through three capacity tiers:

Tier 1: 30 Slots (Basic Wood Chest)

Your starter chest. Craft or buy for 250t. It’s enough for the first season, but you’ll outgrow it fast. Most players end up with 5-10 of these scattered around before upgrading.

Tier 2: 42 Slots (Large Chests)

As of v0.14.0, the Stone, Coral, Obsidian, Breath of Spring chests and Lovely Cottage Icebox were all upgraded to 42 slots. The Stone Storage Chest is the easiest to craft — aim for this one first. The Coral and Obsidian variants are also obtainable by feeding Mimics in the mines (requires the Reclaimer mining perk).

Tier 3: 54 Slots (Deluxe Chests)

The Deluxe Storage Chest (10,000t) and Deluxe Icebox (12,000t) are the largest containers in the game. Both are sold exclusively by Wheedle at the Saturday Market. They’re expensive but well worth it for late-game organization.

Money-saving tip: Don’t rush to buy Deluxe chests. 5 Stone Storage Chests (42 slots each = 210 total slots) cost almost nothing to craft and give you far more total space than one Deluxe chest for a fraction of the price. Wait until money is abundant before upgrading.

Food Storage: Iceboxes Explained

Fields of Mistria has dedicated food storage containers — iceboxes. They work like regular chests but are specifically designed for storing cooked dishes and ingredients.

  • Lovely Cottage Icebox — 42 slots, crafted at Woodcrafting table. Comes in Ash and Oak color variants.
  • Deluxe Icebox — 54 slots, bought from Wheedle at Saturday Market for 12,000t. Available in 5 colors: pink, yellow, green, blue, white.

You don’t have to use iceboxes for food — regular chests work fine. But if you cook a lot and want to separate your kitchen inventory, an icebox next to your stove saves a lot of running back and forth.

6 Storage Management Tips for Fields of Mistria

  1. Color-code your chests — Basic Wood Chests come in 14 color variants. Use green for crops, blue for fish, brown for mining materials, etc. Makes finding things much faster.
  2. Group chests by activity — Put all mining-related chests near the mine entrance, farming chests near your fields, cooking chests in the kitchen. Don’t dump everything in one pile outside your house.
  3. Keep one chest by the Shipping Bin — Fill it with items you plan to sell, then transfer to the bin in bulk. Prevents accidental sales of items you wanted to keep.
  4. Use the Reclaimer mining perk — This skill lets you get free chests (Coral, Obsidian, Mines variants) by feeding Mimics in caves. Huge money saver early on.
  5. Spring Festival = cheap chest — If you score rank 1 or above at the Spring Festival, the souvenir stall sells the Breath of Spring Storage Chest for only 500t (42 slots). Grab it.
  6. Don’t hoard everything — Keep 2-3 of each crop/fish/mineral for quests and gifts. Sell the rest. Storage clutter is a bigger problem than running out of items.

FAQ

Q: How do I move a chest that already has items in it?

A: You can’t — chests must be completely empty before you can pick them up. Empty the chest into your inventory or another chest first, then hit it with a tool to move it.

Q: What’s the fastest way to get a 42-slot chest?

A: Craft the Stone Storage Chest at the Woodcrafting table. If you have the Reclaimer mining perk, feeding Mimics in the Tide Caverns can drop Coral Storage Chests (42 slots) for free.

Q: Is the Deluxe Storage Chest (10,000t) worth it?

A: Only in late game. In early/mid game, crafting multiple 42-slot stone chests gives you more total storage for zero gold. Buy deluxe chests when you have 50,000t+ and nothing else to spend on.

Q: Do iceboxes preserve food longer?

A: No — food doesn’t spoil in Fields of Mistria. Iceboxes are purely for organization. Food stored in regular chests lasts forever.

Q: How many chests should I have by Year 1 Summer?

A: Aim for 8-12 chests total: 2-3 for crops/seeds, 2 for mining materials, 1 for fish, 1 for cooked food, 1 for gifts/quest items, and 2 for overflow. You can always add more.
