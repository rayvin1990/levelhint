Fields of Mistria Mining Guide — Ore Levels, Gems, & Best Perks

Updated: August 2026 • v0.15.x+ • 5 min read
Quick Answer:
  • Unlock: Reach Town Rank 10 (Stone), then talk to Errol via Eiland's letter.
  • 99 floors across 5 biomes. Each 20-floor segment has unique ores and gems.
  • Ore progression: Copper (1-19) → Iron (21-39) → Silver (41-59) → Gold (61-79) → Mistril (81-99).
  • Minimum Copper Pickaxe required to break boulders.
  • Unlock Treasure Hunter first for bonus treasure floors, then ore-doubling perks.

How to Unlock the Mines

The mines become available after:

  1. Reach Town Rank 10 (Stone tier) by donating artifacts, completing requests, and upgrading the town.
  2. You'll receive a letter from Eiland asking you to convince Errol to reopen the mines.
  3. Talk to Errol, complete the conversation, and the mines open permanently.

Perfect variants: Each ore and gem has a rare "Perfect" version. These sell for significantly more and are needed for specific crafting recipes. The Perfect Pick perk (Lv 60) increases their spawn rate.

Ore Node Types

Each mineral appears in two forms:

  • Ore Rock (T1/T2): Common nodes with standard yield. The two visual variants produce the same amount.
  • Ore Chunk: Rich, full-form nodes that yield more ore per break. Prioritize these when you see them.

Pickaxe Requirements

Upgrade your pickaxe as soon as possible. A better pickaxe = fewer swings per rock = more floors cleared per day.

Tips for Efficient Mining Runs

  • Rush to floor 20 fast: The first 20 floors (Upper Mines) are Copper-only. Push through them quickly to reach Iron at floor 21, where profit jumps significantly.
  • Prioritize Ore Chunks: Rich nodes yield more ore per swing than regular rocks. If you see a chunk, mine it first.
  • Treasure floors = free loot: With Treasure Hunter, these special floors spawn randomly and contain high-value chests with furniture, ores, and artifacts. Always fully clear them.
  • Bring food for stamina: Mining consumes stamina per swing. Cooked meals restore more than raw ingredients.
  • Skip the small rocks: Unless you need stone, focus on ore nodes and chunks. Regular rocks yield mostly stone — inefficient for ore runs.
  • Perfect variants sell big: A Perfect Diamond sells for dramatically more than a regular Diamond. Unlock Perfect Pick ASAP.
  • Stone Refiner synergy: Combine mining with the Stone Refiner (unlocked through perks) to process raw ore into refined bars and gems for maximum profit.
