Fields of Mistria Walkthrough — Full Main Story Guide

Every main quest in order, from your first day through the final mine seal and town upgrades.

The whole game in one line: meet the town, restore the mill and the bridge, then push down the mines — breaking the Water, Earth, Fire, and Void seals — while you repair town buildings as your Renown climbs. This guide walks you through each step.

Key Rules That Save You Hours

  • Don’t sell your first copy of anything. Museum donations and seal offerings need rare items you can’t easily re-farm.
  • Renown is the gate. Most town repairs need a specific Renown level, so keep the Request Board moving every day.
  • Seasonal items are one-shot. Miss a seasonal crop or forage and you wait a year — unless Balor’s cart happens to stock it.
  • Fishing and bug nets come early but stay relevant. The Water and Earth seals both need fish and bugs, so keep every unique catch.
