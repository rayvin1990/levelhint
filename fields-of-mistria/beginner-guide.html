Fields of Mistria Beginner Guide — Your First 7 Days, Step-by-Step

Updated: August 2026 • 8 min read
Quick Answer — If you only have 30 seconds:
  • Day 1: Hoe + Watering Can gifted. Plant 15 Turnip seeds. Forage everything in town. Free soup at the Inn for stamina.
  • Day 2: Get the Pickaxe from Olric's "Do a Bro a Favor" quest. Mines unlock — start smelting Copper Ore.
  • Day 3-4: Buy Shovel + Fishing Rod (500t each). Upgrade Backpack (1000t from General Store) ASAP — running out of inventory space is the #1 first-week frustration.
  • Day 5-7: Smelt 4 Copper Ore → 1 Copper Bar → craft Copper Watering Can (1×3 coverage). This single upgrade saves ~1 hour/day of manual watering.
  • First-week rule: Check the Request Board every morning. Forage every tile you walk past. Save free stamina (soup, bathhouse) for mine runs.

What This Guide Covers (and What It Doesn't)

This is a first 7-day walkthrough for new players — focused on tool unlock order, money spending priorities, stamina management, and avoiding early-game mistakes. It is not a full year-by-year strategy guide. After Day 7 you should have enough currency and infrastructure to follow the in-game tutorial prompts naturally.

Day 1 — Starter Tools and First Crops

You start with the Hoe (Celine), Watering Can (Hayden), and a Sword. The axe arrives Day 2, but you can already till soil and plant crops.

Day 1 Priority Checklist

  1. Till 15-20 tiles near your farmhouse. Don't go further out — you water by hand, and walking time is wasted stamina.
  2. Buy Turnip Seeds from the General Store. They cost 25t each, grow in 4 days, sell for 40t. Beginner-friendly.
  3. Water everything. Every tile, every morning, no exceptions until rain handles it for you.
  4. Forage everything in town. Flowers, mushrooms, berries — they regrow on a 2-3 day cycle. Foraging is zero-investment income.
  5. Visit the Sleeping Dragon Inn — interact with the soup cauldron in the center to get a free bowl of Soup of the Day. It restores ~20 stamina. One bowl per day, so don't skip it.
  6. Check the Request Board in Mistria. Take all quests. They don't expire — accept everything and complete as you gather materials naturally.
Day 1 mistake to avoid: Don't plant more than 20 tiles. With a Worn Watering Can (1×1 coverage), 20 tiles = 20 clicks every morning. That's manageable. 50 tiles = frustration and missed watering on rainy days when you're distracted.

Day 2 — Pickaxe + Mines Unlock

Day 2 is the most important day of your first week. Three events happen:

Day 2 Walkthrough

  1. Axe unlocks from Eiland's cutscene. You can now chop wood and clear debris.
  2. Get the Pickaxe from Olric's "Do a Bro a Favor" quest at the Request Board. This unlocks the Mines.
  3. Enter the Mines and mine every Copper Ore node on Floor 1. Copper is the only ore you need for the first two tool upgrades.

Why the Pickaxe is the Single Most Important Day 2 Goal

Every other early-game strategy depends on Copper Ore:

  • Copper Watering Can (1×3 coverage) — smelt 4 Copper Ore into 1 Copper Bar, forge the can at the Blacksmith. Cost: 4 ore + 1,000t.
  • Copper Pickaxe (3×1 mining range) — smelt 4 ore, forge at Blacksmith. Cost: 4 ore + 1,000t.
  • Copper Axe — same recipe. Lets you chop hardwood trees.

For the first Copper upgrade, prioritize the Watering Can. It directly reduces morning watering time by ~60%.

Recipe unlock: Copper Bars need to be smelted at the Furnace first. Place 5 Copper Ore + 1 Coal in the Furnace (one slot, not separate) to get 1 Copper Bar. The Furnace is on your farm, behind the dragon statue.

Day 3-4 — Inventory, Tools, and First Income

Priority Spending Order (First 2,000 Tesserae)

Do not spend in this order randomly. The correct sequence matters:

  1. Backpack upgrade (1,000t) from the General Store. The starter bag is 10 slots. The first upgrade adds 10 more. Running out of inventory space mid-mine-run is the #1 first-week frustration.
  2. Fishing Rod (500t) from the Tackle Shop at the beach. Zero-cost income for the rest of the game. Rainy days are especially profitable — fish don't need watering, you can spend the whole day fishing.
  3. Shovel (500t) from the General Store. Lets you till more tiles and remove debris. Less urgent than the rod, but you'll want it by Day 5.
If you only have 500t on Day 3: Buy the Fishing Rod first. The Backpack upgrade is tempting but you can survive 10 slots for 2-3 more days. Fishing income starts immediately.

Bug Net — Day 4 Free Unlock

On Day 4 (Spring), Luc gives you the Bug Net via the "Something's Bugging Me" quest. The bug net is mostly for museum donations — don't stress about catching every bug right now. Focus on the seasonal bugs for completing insect museum sets (they sell for 50-200t at the museum).

Day 5-7 — Copper Upgrade and Routine

By Day 5, you should have:

  • 15-20 Turnips ready to harvest (Day 4/5 turnaround).
  • 1-2 complete Request Board quest deliveries.
  • ~5-15 Copper Ore from mine runs.
  • ~2,000t from crops + quests + foraged items.

Craft the Copper Watering Can

Smelt 4 Copper Ore → 1 Copper Bar. Forge at the Blacksmith anvil (northwest edge of town). Cost: 1 Copper Bar + 1,000t.

Once equipped, your watering range goes from 1×1 to 1×3. This means a 3×6 crop block takes 6 charged uses instead of 18 single clicks.

Establish Your Daily Routine

By Day 7, your routine should look like:

  1. Wake up, check Request Board (new quests daily).
  2. Water crops with the upgraded Watering Can.
  3. Forage every tile in town you pass through.
  4. Mine run if you have stamina (bring cooked food for refill).
  5. Fish in the afternoon if energy is low (rainy days = no watering needed = pure fishing).
  6. Free soup at Inn in the evening.
  7. Sleep before 1:30 AM — passing out loses stamina recovery the next day.

Free Stamina Refills — Don't Waste Money on Food Yet

There are three free stamina sources available from Day 1. Use them all.

5 Beginner Mistakes That Cost Hours

  1. Planting 50+ tiles on Day 1 with a Worn Watering Can. By Day 3 you'll be skipping watering sessions and losing crops. Fix: Stay under 20 tiles until you have the Copper Watering Can.
  2. Ignoring the Request Board. Quests give 50-1,000t + Renown (unlocks Town Rank prizes). Skipping them means slower progression. Fix: Take every quest every morning. They never expire.
  3. Buying the Shovel before the Backpack upgrade. The 10-slot bag fills up fast. Mining runs end with you dropping half the loot on the ground. Fix: Backpack upgrade first.
  4. Selling ore instead of smelting. 4 Copper Ore sells for ~80t. Smelted into 1 Copper Bar + forged into a Copper Pickaxe, you save 1,000t on tool upgrades. Fix: Always smelt before selling.
  5. Not upgrading to Copper Watering Can before upgrading farmland. Players try to expand first, then realize they can't water the bigger plot. Fix: Upgrade watering can first, then till more tiles.

What Comes After Day 7?

Once your routine is established and the Copper Watering Can is in hand, the game's natural progression takes over:

  • Week 2: Iron tools unlock. Upgrade watering can to Iron (3×3 coverage) when affordable.
  • Week 3-4: First Greenhouse materials. Town Rank milestones. Animal barn construction.
  • Month 2: Romance options open up (see our Romance Guide).
  • End-game: Mine all 99 floors, complete all museum sets, max Town Rank.
