Fields of Mistria Update Log & Patch Notes

Full version history from Early Access to 1.0 — updated August 2026.

Quick answer:
  • Current version: v1.0.4 (August 19, 2026) — bug fixes including Stillwell quest tracking.
  • 1.0 full release: August 5, 2026 — marriage, weddings, children, full town restoration, Steam achievements, and 7 new languages.
  • The game spent 2 years in Early Access with 4 Major Updates (Nov 2024, Mar 2025, Jul 2025, Feb 2026).
  • Jump to: Latest Patch · Major Updates · Full Version Table

Latest Patch — v1.0.4 (August 19, 2026)

Bug-fix patch addressing quest tracking issues with Stillwell tasks and other fixes. For line-by-line details, see the official Steam news hub.

1.0 Full Release (August 5, 2026)

The headline update — Fields of Mistria left Early Access exactly two years after entering it:

  • Marriage is here: 10-heart proposal events, weddings, and children.
  • Town restoration storyline can be fully completed — Town Renown cap raised to 100.
  • Museum can be fully completed, with new Mistril-tier town events.
  • Steam achievements added (retroactively unlocked when loading an old save).
  • 7 languages in Beta: Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, French, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Spanish.
  • New quests: Expand the Saturday Market, Repair the Bell Tower. Stillwell and Zorel join the Saturday Market.

The 4 Major Updates (Early Access Era)

Major Update 1 — v0.12.0 (November 18, 2024)

  • Rideable horses and mount skill bonuses.
  • Six-heart events for romance candidates; Shooting Star Festival (Summer 28).
  • Crown Requests (special order type), two home upgrades, 21 new Tier 4 skill perks.
  • New mines monsters: Essence Bat and Mimic.

Major Update 2 — v0.13.0 (March 10, 2025)

  • Caldarus revealed; Lava Caves biome and the Deep Woods area.
  • Pet system (pets can be assigned to collect resources) and the Animal Festival (Winter 10).
  • Water Sprite Statue (auto-waters crops) and Animal Sprite Statue.
  • Farm expansion purchases; Stone Refinery town restoration project.

Major Update 3 — v0.14.0 (July 21, 2025)

  • 8-heart events: choose "best friends" or "dating" with candidates (marriage still not in game yet).
  • Weekend date system with collectible photo cards.
  • Harvest Festival (Fall 10) with the Queen Berry contest; new Saturday Market vendors Taliferro and Wheedle.
  • Greenhouse unlocked via quest — year-round crops.

Major Update 4 — v0.15.0 (February 23, 2026)

  • "Breaking The Ruins Seal" questline and the Ancient Ruins mines biome — the mines storyline can be fully completed.
  • A secret romanceable bachelorette unlocked by finishing the mines storyline.
  • Beehives (flowers in, honey out) and vivariums (insects in, bait/pheromones out).
  • Lost and Found box (weekly free items); Mistril tools and equipment (current top tier).

How Updates Roll Out

Steam updates the game automatically. If an update stalls: right-click the game in your Library → Properties → Installed Files → Verify integrity of game files.
