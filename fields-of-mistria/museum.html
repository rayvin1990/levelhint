Fields of Mistria Museum Guide

How the 4 wings work, and how to fill the museum without wasting a single rare item.

Quick answer:
  • The museum has 4 wings: Archaeology, Fish, Flora, and Insects.
  • Each wing is split into sets; completing a set pays furniture, money, and Renown.
  • Donate the first copy of everything — most items are one-time donations and a pain to re-farm.
  • Check the Almanac to see if an item is still needed before you sell or use it.

The 4 Wings

1. Archaeology

Dig up circular dig spots with your shovel or pickaxe — on the surface and in the mines. Some sets need a skill first (like Well Placed, Aquatic Antiquities, Sunken Secrets, Lost to History, Mist Sight, Former Farmers), so a few collections only appear once you unlock the matching perk. Artifacts span Dig Site materials, prehistoric fossils, ancient relics, mine sets, and the Perfect ore/gem sets.

2. Fish

Organized by location and season, not alphabetically. Each season has River, Pond, and Ocean sets, plus mine sets (Upper Mines, Tide Caverns, Deep Earth, Lava Caves, Ruins), Fish Trap and Bait sets — and the Legendary Fish set (needs Fishing lv 30 + the Legendary perk). See the Legendary Fish guide for those four.

3. Flora

Tied to seasonal farming and foraging. Each season has Crop, Flower, and Forage sets, plus Deep Woods and mine forage sets. Miss a seasonal crop or flower and you wait a year (unless Balor’s cart stocks it). The Void Forage set also needs the Void Sight skill.

4. Insects

Caught with your bug net across seasons, weather, and times of day. Plus the Bee, Honey, and Bug Pheromone sets (from Apiaries and Terrariums, added in 1.0), and the Legendary Insects set. Bug collecting doesn’t need a skill unlock, unlike fish and artifacts.

How to Fill It Efficiently

  1. Donate the first copy of everything. Selling something the museum still needs is the #1 mistake — rare items are one-time and slow to re-farm.
  2. Check the Almanac before you sell. Every item’s tooltip shows whether it’s already donated, so confirm before it hits the shipping bin.
  3. Do seasonal items first. Crops, fish, flowers, and bugs are season-gated — sweep them before the season ends, or wait a full year.
  4. Unlock the reveal skills early. Several artifact sets won’t even spawn until you have Well Placed, Aquatic Antiquities, and similar perks — grab them in the skill tree when you can.
  5. Revisit deep mine floors. Rare gems and artifacts only appear at specific depths, so return to the deep levels regularly.
Museum and Town Rank feed each other. Every donation adds Renown, which raises Town Rank, which unlocks more of the game. Treat the museum as a side quest that quietly pays you the whole run.
