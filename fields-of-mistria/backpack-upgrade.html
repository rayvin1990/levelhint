Fields of Mistria Backpack Upgrade Guide

How to increase your inventory space from 10 slots to 30.

Quick answer:
  • Buy the Bag Upgrade from the General Store in Mistria (Tools section).
  • Upgrade 1 costs 1,000 Tesserae → 10 → 20 slots.
  • Upgrade 2 costs 2,000 Tesserae → 20 → 30 slots, but only after the General Store upgrade quest.
  • Switch between hotbar tabs with the Tab key.

Where to Buy the Backpack Upgrade

You start with 10 inventory slots. To expand, go to the General Store in the town of Mistria (north of your farm, toward the town center). Interact with the checkout counter, open the Tools section, and buy the Bag Upgrade.

The first upgrade doubles your inventory from 10 to 20 slots. It also adds a second hotbar that you swap between by pressing Tab — the extra slots aren’t all on one screen, they live in a second tab.

When to Buy It

The first upgrade is technically available on day 1, but you start with only 50 Tesserae. Don’t grind foraging to afford it early — you’ll make far more money by investing your first profits into seeds and tools. A good rule: wait until your first crop harvest, then save the 1,000 for the bag.

For the second upgrade, finish the General Store upgrade quest first — it won’t appear in the shop until you do.

How to Get Tesserae Faster

  • Grow and sell crops — the reliable core income. Turnips early, strawberries and other regrowables later.
  • Complete Request Board quests — they pay Tesserae and raise Renown, which comes with its own cash bonuses.
  • Forage — berries and flowers stack and cost no stamina.
  • Sell spare resources — Fiber, Hay, and Wood you aren’t using turn into quick cash.
Tip: If you’re heading into the Mines, a bigger bag matters more than almost anywhere else — you’ll be carrying tools, food, and loot on one trip. Get the first upgrade before long mining runs.
