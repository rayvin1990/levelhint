Fields of Mistria Magic & Spells Guide

All 4 spells, how to unlock each one, and how Mana works.

Quick answer:
  • There are 4 spells: Full Restore, Summon Rain, Growth, and Dragon’s Breath.
  • Reach Mine Floor 10, then sleep — Caldarus grants your first spell (Full Restore) and a Mana point.
  • Each spell after that comes from breaking a Seal every 20 floors (20, 40, 60).
  • Spells cost Mana, which refills slowly — a bit each night you sleep, or instantly with a Mana Potion.

How to Unlock Magic

  1. Open the Mines (reach Town Rank 10, then meet Errol and Eiland at the entrance).
  2. Dig down to Floor 10, then return home and sleep — Caldarus speaks to you and grants Full Restore plus your first Mana point.
  3. From there, break the Water Seal (Floor 20), Earth Seal (Floor 40), and Fire Seal (Floor 60) — each gives a new spell and another Mana point.

How Mana Works

  • Casting costs Mana. Each spell has a cost (1–2 Mana); if you’re out, you can’t cast.
  • Mana refills slowly. Sleeping restores about a quarter of a Mana point each night, so it can take a few days to come back.
  • Mana Potions restore it instantly, but they’re rare — found in loot chests, not crafted or bought.
  • One active spell at a time. Pin a spell in the Spells tab (sparkle icon in your Journal) to put it on your hotbar.

Which Spells Actually Matter

  • Summon Rain is the MVP. It waters your whole farm instantly — the single biggest time-saver in the game once your plot gets big.
  • Growth is the crop accelerator. Use it to fast-track a 3×3 patch or push trees a stage ahead when you need fruit sooner.
  • Full Restore is your emergency button. Great for long mine runs — pop it before you faint.
  • Dragon’s Breath is mostly a key. It clears blocked paths and the Fire Seal cave; handy in the mines, situational on the farm.
Cast between crop patches. Mana is scarce, so don’t waste Summon Rain or Growth on a half-empty field. Wait until your crops are all planted, then cast once to cover everything.
