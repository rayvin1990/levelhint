Fields of Mistria Crispy Fried Earthshroom Recipe — How to Get & Cook It

Updated: August 2026 • 5 min read
Quick Answer:
  • Ingredients: Earthshroom ×1, Flour ×1, Oil ×1
  • Requirement: Cooking Level 14 + any Kitchen Tier 1 or higher
  • Recipe location: Treasure chest in Tide Caverns (need Taste Maker cooking skill)
  • Effect: +66 Health, +66 Stamina — snack category
  • Sell price: 250t

The Crispy Fried Earthshroom is a 2-star snack dish in Fields of Mistria. It’s one of the few recipes that can’t be bought from the tavern or learned by leveling up — you have to physically find the recipe scroll inside the Tide Caverns dungeon. Here’s exactly how to get it and why it’s worth your time.

How to Unlock the Recipe

Unlike most cooking recipes that you buy from the Inn or unlock by leveling up Cooking, the Crispy Fried Earthshroom recipe is obtained through exploration:

  1. Unlock Tide Caverns — This is one of the later mines. You need to have progressed deep enough into the mining content to reach the water-themed cavern zone.
  2. Get the Taste Maker skill — In the Cooking skill tree, unlock the Taste Maker perk. This skill causes cooking recipe scrolls to drop from treasure chests and Mimics in the mines.
  3. Find the recipe scroll — With Taste Maker active, explore Tide Caverns and search every treasure chest. The recipe scroll can also drop from feeding Mimics in this zone.
  4. Learn it — Use the recipe scroll from your inventory. Once learned, the dish appears permanently in your cooking menu.
Lore tidbit: According to NPC Taliferro, "Rumors say the original recipe was found by a Taste Maker inside a treasure chest... while exploring a Water-filled Cavern." The game literally tells you where to look.

Is Crispy Fried Earthshroom Worth Making?

Short answer: Yes, but not for the stats. At +66 HP/Stamina, it’s decent but not outstanding for its Cooking Level 14 requirement. The real value is in gifting.

If you’re working on friendship with Hemlock, this dish is one of his favorite gifts. Craft a batch of 5-10 and gift them across multiple days for maximum heart gain.

Speed Cooking Tips

With a base cooking time of 30 minutes, making multiple Earthshrooms takes a while. Use the Time To Eat I/II/III cooking perks to reduce crafting time by 10/20/30 minutes respectively. At Time To Eat III, cooking is instant (0 minutes).

Before you grind for this recipe: Cooking Level 14 takes a significant time investment. You need to cook hundreds of dishes to reach that level. If you’re below Cooking 10, focus on leveling first with cheap recipes like Bread or Jam before chasing dungeon recipes.

FAQ

Q: Can I get the recipe without the Taste Maker skill?

A: Unlikely. The recipe scroll drops from chests and Mimics in Tide Caverns, and without Taste Maker (Cooking perk), these drops are extremely rare or don’t happen at all.

Q: What’s the fastest way to reach Cooking Level 14?

A: Spam-cook the simplest recipes: Bread (Flour ×1) or Blackberry Jam (Blackberry ×6 + Sugar ×1). Both require minimal ingredients and have low level requirements. Cook in bulk and sell the results.

Q: Can I grow Earthshrooms on my farm?

A: No. Earthshrooms only grow inside Tide Caverns. You’ll need to revisit the caverns each time you want more.

Q: Is it better to sell the dish or gift it?

A: Gift to Hemlock first (friendship points are harder to grind than gold). Once you’ve maxed his hearts, selling at 250t each is a decent side income if you have excess Earthshrooms.
