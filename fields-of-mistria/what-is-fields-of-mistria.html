What Is Fields of Mistria?

A quick overview before you buy — what it is, how it plays, and whether it’s for you.

Fields of Mistria is a farming and life-sim RPG from NPC Studio (Chicago indie dev). You inherit an overgrown homestead in the village of Mistria, and you rebuild it after an earthquake while magic slowly returns to the land. Think Stardew Valley with anime charm and actual magic.

What You Actually Do

  • Farm — grow crops by season, raise animals, breed rare color variants.
  • Fish, mine, craft — the classic loop, plus a magic system layered on top.
  • Restore the town — repair the mill, bridge, and more as your Renown climbs.
  • Explore the mines — descend through four biomes, break four elemental seals.
  • Romance — 12 marriage candidates, heart events, dating, marriage, and kids (added in 1.0).
  • Complete the museum — hundreds of collectibles across four wings.

Who It’s For

  • Fans of Stardew Valley, Harvest Moon, Rune Factory — it’s the same genre, done well.
  • People who want a cozy, low-pressure game — no failure states, play at your own pace.
  • Anyone who liked the 90s anime aesthetic — the art is the game’s signature.

How It’s Different From Stardew

  • Magic is core, not cosmetic. Spells water your crops, fast-grow them, and open paths.
  • No combat pressure. The mines have combat, but it’s lighter and more forgiving.
  • Retro anime style — pixel art with 80s/90s anime character portraits.

The Numbers

  • 97% positive on Steam (over 28,000 reviews).
  • 1 million+ copies sold by the 1.0 launch.
  • 1.0 released August 2026 — added marriage, kids, and finished the main story.
  • Platforms: PC (Steam), with Steam Deck support.
