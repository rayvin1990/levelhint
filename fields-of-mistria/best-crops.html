Fields of Mistria Best Crops — Most Profitable Plants by Season (Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter)

Updated: August 2026 • 6 min read
Quick Answer — If you only have 30 seconds:
  • Spring: Strawberry (regrows, 300t seed → 125t/harvest every 3 days). Plant before the 15th. After the 15th, switch to Cabbage (70t → 180t, 9 days).
  • Summer: Tomato / Corn / Tea (300t seed, regrow every 3 days). Watermelon if planting after mid-month (70t → 180t).
  • Fall: Onion (300t seed, 135t sell, regrows) — the highest-gold regrowable in the game. Pumpkin for late-month planting.
  • Winter: Snow Peas (regrow), Daikon Radish (single-harvest).
  • Greenhouse: Strawberry + Snow Peas — produces year-round with no seasonal restrictions.
  • Golden rule: Regrowable crops (300t seeds) beat single-harvest crops (70t seeds) over a full season — but only if you plant them before the 15th of the month.

How Crop Profit Works in Fields of Mistria

Fields of Mistria has two profit models for crops, and picking the wrong one costs thousands of Tesserae per season.

  • Single-harvest crops (Cabbage, Watermelon, Pumpkin, Daikon Radish): Plant once, harvest once, done. Low seed cost (25-70t), sell price 40-180t, growth time 4-9 days.
  • Regrowable crops (Strawberry, Corn, Tea, Tomato, Onion, Cranberry, Snow Peas): Plant once, harvest repeatedly every 3 days after first maturity. Higher seed cost (300t), sell price 125-150t.
The 15th-of-month rule: A regrowable crop planted on Day 1 of a season achieves 8 harvests over 28 days. The same crop planted on Day 15 achieves only 4 harvests — still profitable, but barely beats a Cabbage-equivalent. After the 15th, switch to single-harvest crops.

Best Greenhouse Crops

Once you unlock the Greenhouse (Carpenter's Shop upgrade, Town Rank 75), you can grow any crop year-round inside it — regardless of season. This changes the crop meta completely.

3 Crop Mistakes That Cost Thousands

  1. Planting regrowables after the 15th. A Strawberry planted on Day 16 yields only 3 harvests (375t) minus 300t seed cost = 75t profit over 13 days. That's 5.7 t/day — less than Cabbage (12.2 t/day). After mid-month, always plant single-harvest.
  2. Buying Cabbage-equivalents in Spring Year 1, Day 1. Cabbage takes 9 days, and you have no backup income on Day 1. Plant Turnips first (4-day turnaround), pocket the profit, THEN buy Cabbage/Strawberry.
  3. Selling crops raw when cooking nearly doubles profit. Bread (Flour ×1 = 190t), Quiche (Egg + Milk + Flour = 270t), Baked Sweetroot (Sweetroot = stamina food). If you have the kitchen time, always cook before selling. See our Cooking Guide for the full recipe-to-profit breakdown.
