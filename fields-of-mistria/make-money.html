Fields of Mistria: How to Make Money Fast — 7 Best Ways to Earn Tesserae

Updated: August 2026 • 6 min read
Quick Answer — If you only have 30 seconds:
  • Day 1-7: Complete request board quests (50-1,000t each) + forage berries + plant Turnips (4-day growth, 40t sell).
  • Week 2+: Buy a fishing rod (Tackle Shop, beach). Fish when you run out of energy.
  • Mid-game: Cook ingredients before selling (recipes sell for more). Craft Copper tools at the forge (480t each).
  • End-game: Plant regrowable crops + mine for gems (Ruby, Sapphire).

7 Best Ways to Make Money — Ranked by Priority

1. Complete Request Board Quests BEST EARLY

From day one, the Request Board in town gives you simple fetch quests: bring an NPC a crop, a fish, or a foraged item, and they pay 50-1,000t. No investment required — just pick up the quest, gather what they asked for, deliver it.

Bonus: completing quests also gives Renown which increases your Town Rank. Each new Town Rank rewards you with a prize chest containing Tesserae, accessories, or crafting materials.

2. Grow & Sell Crops CORE INCOME

Farming is your main money engine. Plant seeds, water daily, harvest, repeat. The key is reinvesting: use crop profits to buy more seeds.

Early game crop strategy: Spend ALL your starting Tesserae on Turnip Seeds, plant them, harvest, sell, and buy even more seeds. Repeat until you have 4-5 full plots. By week 2 you'll be making 500-1,000t per harvest cycle.

3. Fishing ZERO COST

Buy a fishing rod from Terithia at the Tackle Shop (beach). Fishing costs no resources, uses only energy, and fish sell for 20-100t each. Higher rarity = more money.

Best strategy: when you run out of energy from farming/foraging, go to the Inn and grab free soup (restores stamina once per day), then fish until 1:00 AM. No investment = pure profit.

4. Cooking for Profit MID GAME

Cooked dishes sell for more than the sum of their ingredients. This is free money if you have the kitchen time.

  • Bread: Flour ×1 (cheap) → 190t sell. Spam it.
  • Summer Salad: ~65t profit over raw ingredients, +120 HP/Stamina.
  • Quiche: Egg + Milk + Flour → 270t. Solid mid-game profit.

See our Cooking Guide for the full breakdown.

5. Mining & Gems MID-LATE

Unlock the Mines at Stone Town Rank. Inside you'll find:

  • Ore deposits: Copper, Iron, Silver. Smelt into ingots, craft tools (Copper tool = 480t sell).
  • Gems: Ruby, Sapphire. High value, sell directly. Some gems sell for hundreds of tesserae.
  • Monster drops: Enemies drop small amounts of Tesserae (5-50t each). Not a main income source, but adds up over a long mine run.
Mining profit loop: Go in with 10-15 cooked dishes for stamina. Mine until your inventory is full. Smelt ores into ingots, craft the highest-value tools you can, sell everything. A good mine run can net 2,000-5,000t.

6. Town Rank Rewards PASSIVE

Every time your Town Rank increases, claim your reward from the chest next to the Request Board. Rewards include:

  • Direct Tesserae payouts
  • Valuable items you can sell
  • Crafting materials that save you money

This is passive — do quests + sell items through the Shipping Bin, and you'll rank up naturally.

Daily Money Routine (Optimized)

  1. Morning: Water and harvest crops. Check Request Board for new quests.
  2. Midday: Forage along your route. Complete any quests. Visit shops.
  3. Afternoon: Fish or mine (whichever you've invested in). Use Inn soup for extra stamina.
  4. Evening: Cook harvested crops into dishes. Dump everything into the Shipping Bin.
  5. Night: Go to sleep. Next morning: collect overnight shipping profits. Reinvest in seeds.
Golden rule of Mistria money: Never end a day with Tesserae sitting in your wallet. Before sleeping, spend ALL your money on seeds (morning) or other investments. Cash in wallet = cash not growing. A Tesserae spent on a Turnip seed today becomes 40t in 4 days.

FAQ

Q: How much money should I have by the end of Year 1 Spring?

A: A well-optimized first spring should net you 5,000-10,000t in total earnings. Spend most on seeds, kitchen, and a backpack upgrade.

Q: Should I sell or donate my first artifact/fish/crop?

A: Donate the first one to the Museum. Duplicates can be sold. Museum donations unlock rewards and the collection sets give bonuses later.

Q: What's the single fastest way to get money in day 1?

A: Grab every Request Board quest you can complete with foraged items (berries, flowers). Each pays 50-200t. Plant Turnips with your starting 50t. Forage everything on the map before nightfall and sell it.

Q: Are animals profitable?

A: Not in the short term. Chickens take time to start laying and the feed costs eat into early profits. Focus on crops first. Buy animals in Year 1 Summer or later when you have a cash buffer.
