Fields of Mistria Legendary Fish Guide

All 4 legendary fish, their exact spawn conditions, and how to unlock them.

Quick answer:
  • Unlock the Legendary fishing perk (Fishing lv 30, 80 Essence at Caldarus’ Statue) to make legendary fish spawn.
  • On a matching season + weather day, the game says “A legendary fish is nearby…” at the start of the day.
  • There are 4 legendary fish: Cherry (Spring), Lightning (Summer), Leaf (Fall), Snow (Winter).
  • Each can only be caught once per save. Donate the first copy to the Museum.

How to Unlock Legendary Fish

Legendary fish are gated by a skill perk, not a special rod. Here’s the full chain:

  1. Raise your Fishing skill to level 30 so the Tier 3 perks unlock.
  2. Spend 80 Essence on the Legendary perk at Caldarus’ Statue on your farm.
  3. Once you have the perk, check the start of each day. On a valid season + weather day, the morning notice “A legendary fish is nearby…” appears with the fish’s silhouette.

Any fishing rod works — all four are caught with a standard pole. Better rods only cast farther, so you don’t need Gold, Silver, or Mistril rods to land them.

Each Fish in Detail

Cherry Fish — Spring

Appears in spring on the breezy day with cherry blossoms. Look in pond water for a small shadow. Pond water exists in Mistria, the Eastern Road, and the Deep Woods — the large Eastern Road pond is the easiest to scan because shadows are easy to see and you can leave the map to refresh them.

Lightning Fish — Summer

Appears in summer during storm or thunderstorm weather — ordinary rain is not enough. Search river water for a medium shadow. The Narrows is a good first stop because the river is easy to scan, but any river water (farm, Mistria, Eastern Road, Narrows) works.

Leaf Fish — Fall

Appears in fall on the breezy day with falling leaves. Look in river water for a small shadow. Same rule as the Lightning Fish: the condition is the water type, not one specific map.

Snow Fish — Winter

Appears in winter during a blizzard — ordinary snow is not the same condition. Check ocean water for a medium shadow. Ocean water is reachable at the Beach, Western Ruins, and Sweetwater Farm; the Beach is usually easiest because of the open shoreline.

How to Refresh Fish Shadows

Once the morning notice confirms a legendary fish can appear, go to the right water type and look only for the listed shadow size. If the right shadow isn’t there, leave the map and re-enter it to refresh the fish in that area. Pick a spot with a short exit path, check the shadows, then reset until the right size appears. This is a player-tested tactic, so expect some bad rolls.

What to Do With Legendary Fish

Critical: each legendary fish can only be caught once per save file. Donate your first copy to the Museum’s Fish Wing immediately — selling or spending the only copy blocks the Legendary set. Each sells for 500 Tesserae, but museum completion is worth more than the cash.

The four legendary fish are also used in the late Crown Request named “Fish.” Finish the Museum’s Legendary set first, then decide whether the request is worth giving up a copy.

Common Mistakes

  • Treating ordinary rain as a storm. The Lightning Fish needs the heavy storm/thunderstorm weather class, not light rain.
  • Fishing the wrong shadow size. Cherry and Leaf Fish are small; Lightning and Snow Fish are medium. Ignore the other sizes or you’ll waste casts.
  • Forgetting the perk. Legendary fish never spawn until you buy the Legendary perk (lv 30, 80 Essence).
  • Selling instead of donating. There is no second chance — one catch per save.
