Fields of Mistria Romance & Marriage Guide — All 12 Candidates

Updated: August 2026 • v1.0.0+ • 5 min read
Quick Answer:
  • Total romance candidates: 12 (6 bachelorettes, 6 bachelors)
  • Marriage added in v1.0.0. You can only marry one candidate per save.
  • Best Friend route available as an alternative to romance (since v0.14.0).
  • Marriage unlocks 3 pieces of your spouse's furniture for your farmhouse.
  • Build friendship via daily talking, gifts, and festivals — then ask them on dates.

How Romance Works

Romance in Fields of Mistria is available with any villager marked as "single" in their Social tab. The path goes:

  1. Friendship: Talk daily, give liked/loved gifts, and attend festivals to raise heart levels.
  2. Dating: Once your friendship is high enough, you can ask the candidate on dates (Elsie provides a tutorial).
  3. Heart Events: Each candidate has unique cutscenes that trigger at specific heart thresholds and locations.
  4. Marriage: Available in v1.0.0+. Propose to a candidate after reaching max hearts. A wedding event follows (roadmap confirmed, details TBA).
Important: Marriage is exclusive — you can only marry one candidate at a time. It's unknown whether divorce exists (not confirmed as of v0.15.x). Choose carefully, or save before proposing!

Best Friend Route (Non-Romance Alternative)

Since v0.14.0, you can choose the "Best Friend" path with any candidate instead of romance. This builds deep platonic bonds without dating/marriage. Useful if you want to befriend everyone without romantic complications or you're waiting to pick one spouse.

Marriage Rewards

Once married (v1.0.0+), you unlock:

  • 3 spouse-specific furniture pieces to decorate your farmhouse
  • A wedding event with cutscenes (roadmap-confirmed, specifics TBA)
  • Your spouse moves into your farmhouse

Tips for Fast Romance

  • Give loved gifts daily: Each candidate has unique preferences. Experiment or check the wiki for exact loved item lists.
  • Talk every day: Even without a gift, daily conversation builds friendship points.
  • Birthdays matter: Giving a loved gift on a candidate's birthday multiplies the friendship gain.
  • Attend festivals: Many festivals have interactions that boost relationship points with multiple characters at once.
  • Heart events trigger at specific times/locations: If a heart event isn't firing, try visiting their usual spot at different times of day.
