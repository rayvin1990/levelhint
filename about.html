About LevelHint

LevelHint is an independent, fan-made game guide site. We exist for one reason: to give you the exact answer you searched for, without the filler.

Why “no fluff”?

Most game guides make you scroll through three paragraphs of backstory before they tell you what you came for. We write the other way — the answer first, then the details. If you’re mid-game and stuck, you shouldn’t have to hunt for the one line that matters.

What we cover

  • Fields of Mistria — farm layout, best crops by season, money routes, fishing, mining, romance, and more.
  • ReStory — make money fast, device repair tips, customer stories, licenses & bills.

How we keep guides accurate

We play the games, then cross-check every number and mechanic against official wikis, patch notes, and community sources like Steam forums and Reddit. When something comes from a single community source rather than the game itself, we flag it. If you find an error, tell us and we’ll fix it.

Who we are

LevelHint is run by a small independent team. We’re not a publisher, not a wiki farm, and not affiliated with any game developer. We just like solving games and writing down what works.
